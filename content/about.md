# Pierre-Henry Soria

{{< figure src="/images/pierre-henry-soria.jpg" alt="Pierre-Henry Soria playing a compact keyboard beside a window" title="Pierre-Henry Soria" caption="Pierre-Henry Soria at home, making time for music and reflection." >}}

I am a passionate problem-solving and product engineer with a strong interest in open-source ecosystems and data science. I enjoy building and shipping software that turns useful ideas into clear, reliable products.

## How I Work

My work sits at the intersection of engineering, product thinking, and practical problem solving. I care about thoughtful systems, the craft of software, and making steady progress from an idea through to something people can use.

I also write and journal to clarify ideas, document what I learn, and share perspectives on technology, intentional work, and building a meaningful life.

## Outside Software

I am a nature lover with a focus on reading non-fiction, life optimisation, minimalism, and self-sufficiency. Time outdoors, thoughtful books, and smaller, well-designed routines help me keep a clear perspective while working on ambitious projects.

## Small Pleasures

I enjoy Roquefort and other great cheeses, a good coffee (usually an almond flat white or ristretto), and dark chocolate.
