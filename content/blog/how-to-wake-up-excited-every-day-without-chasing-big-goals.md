**Wake up to your own wonder.**

There’s a certain magic in life that’s easy to overlook. It’s not hidden in faraway places or reserved for the lucky few. It’s right here, in the things that make your heart race and your mind buzz with anticipation. Think about those mornings when you wake up before the alarm, eyes wide open, because something thrilling is waiting for you. Maybe it’s a trip to Miami, a wedding day, the birth of your child, or simply the promise of a new adventure. That spark, that’s what I want to talk about.

## The Wonders That Make Us Come Alive

The wonders of life aren’t just grand events. They’re the passions that pull you out of bed, the projects that keep you up late, the dreams that refuse to let you rest. When you find what excites you, everything else falls into place. Your health improves. Your energy soars. Your relationships deepen. It’s as if the universe hands you a package deal: happiness, fulfillment, and a reason to keep moving forward.

> *Happiness is not something ready made. It comes from your own actions.*
>
> — Dalai Lama

## Building a Life Around What Moves You

Imagine crafting a job that lets you live on your own terms. A job that keeps you tuned in to the world, always listening, always learning. That’s what I’ve aimed for. Right now, I’m knee-deep in a new startup project, tinkering with scripts and ideas. Even after building hundreds of apps and working as a senior engineer and tech lead, I still crave new challenges. I love my work, my colleagues, and the developers I manage. But I need more. I need to stay awake to possibility.

{{< figure src="https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1602891867080-1d56348202a3?crop=entropy&cs=tinysrgb&fit=max&fm=jpg&ixid=M3w2NjcyMjF8MHwxfHNlYXJjaHw3fHxmcmVzaCUyMGNvZmZlZSUyMGNvenklMjBiZWR8ZW58MHwwfHx8MTc4MjAzOTU3OHww&ixlib=rb-4.1.0&q=80&w=1080" alt="Savoring mornings, one quiet moment" title="How to Wake Up Excited Every Day Without Chasing Big Goals" caption="Savoring mornings, one quiet moment - Photo by [Amin Hasani](https://unsplash.com/@aminhasani)" >}}

Artificial intelligence fascinates me. If one day AI replaces my job, so be it. Maybe coding as we know it will disappear, but the art of solving complex problems might stick around a bit longer. And if not? Well, I’ll make cheese in the Swiss mountains. Cheese is my second passion. I can see myself living the simple life, under the stars, with my partner, savoring every moment.

## Curiosity: The Secret Ingredient

I’m always juggling new projects. Data science, for example, keeps my mind sharp. I hold a master’s in it, and I’m constantly learning, experimenting, and growing. Curiosity is essential. It keeps you alert, open, and ready for whatever comes next.

> *Stay hungry, stay foolish.*
>
> — Steve Jobs

## The Power of Focus

Let’s be honest: it’s easy to get distracted. Conversations drift, projects lose their shape, and before you know it, you’re off track. I’ve noticed this in myself, too. It’s important to stay focused, whether you’re talking with someone or building something new. Think of it like a laser, zeroed in on a single point. That’s how you make real progress, by pouring your energy into one thing at a time.

> *Success is the sum of small efforts, repeated day in and day out.*
>
> — Robert Collier

## Find Your Ray of Sunshine

In the end, what matters most is finding something that lights you up. Something that gives you a permanent ray of sunshine, no matter how cloudy the day. That’s what I wish for you: to excel at something that makes you feel alive, every single day.

---

## Key Takeaways

- *Wake up for what excites you, not just what you must do.*
- *Build your life around your passions, not just your obligations.*
- *Stay curious, never stop learning or exploring new interests.*
- *Focus on one thing at a time for real progress.*
- *Let your happiness fuel your health, energy, and relationships.*

## Reflection

- *What truly makes you want to jump out of bed in the morning?*
- *Are you investing enough in your own happiness?*
- *What’s one passion you’ve neglected that you could bring back into your life?*
