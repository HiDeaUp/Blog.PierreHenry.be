**Everyday Self-Awareness**

## How Tracking My Habits Changed My Life, One Day at a Time

### Discovering the power of small, honest choices and daily rituals to build a life that feels genuinely fulfilling.

Most people imagine a beautiful life as something grand—an achievement waiting at the end of a long, arduous journey. But I’ve learned that the real secret is much simpler: it’s hidden in the small, conscious choices we make every day. The life I love didn’t arrive all at once; it’s the result of paying close attention to my daily routines and decisions.

Here’s a concrete example. Recently, I was at my computer, coffee in hand, working on an update for my app, Mil Snap. This isn’t a promotion—just context. Mil Snap is a food tracking app I created for iPhone, designed to help people (myself included) become more aware of what they eat and drink. Every time I have a meal, snack, or even just a coffee, I snap a photo and the app logs it automatically. Over time, it builds a detailed history of everything I’ve consumed—healthy, ultra-processed, or somewhere in between.

What surprised me most was how much my perception of my own habits was off. For example, I never realized how many fries I’d eaten over the past five years until I saw the numbers: 100 kilograms, maybe even 300. That’s staggering. Five years can feel both long and short, but when you see the cumulative impact of your choices laid out so clearly, it’s impossible to ignore.

This kind of transparency changed everything for me. It’s easy to forget what you ate last week, let alone last year. Our memories are selective, and we tend to minimize the less healthy choices. But with a tool like Mil Snap, there’s no hiding. I can see exactly where I’m overdoing it—like with those fries—and where I’m doing well. It’s not about guilt or perfection, but about honest self-awareness.

## The Joy of Consistent Progress

Developing this app became more than just a technical project; it turned into a daily ritual that grounds me. Every day, I dedicate at least half an hour—sometimes up to an hour and a half—to improving Mil Snap. Even on the busiest days, I carve out that time. The progress might seem small in the moment, but over weeks and months, it adds up to something meaningful.

This daily commitment brings me genuine pleasure and a sense of personal pride. It’s not just about building an app; it’s about building a life that feels right for me. Showing up for myself, day after day, makes all the difference. I encourage you to find something that matters to you and make it a non-negotiable part of your routine.

## Building a Life You Love

Life is too short to waste on things that bore or drain us. We owe it to ourselves to pursue what lights us up, even if it’s just for a few minutes each day. The cumulative effect is powerful. Whether it’s tracking your habits, learning a new skill, or working on a passion project, the key is consistency and self-honesty.

For me, Mil Snap is more than an app—it’s a mirror. It reflects not only what I eat and drink, but also how I choose to spend my time and energy. It reminds me that every small decision matters, and that the life I want is built one day at a time.

If you’re searching for a way to create a life you truly love, start by paying attention to the details. Embrace transparency, commit to small daily actions, and don’t underestimate the power of incremental progress. Life is too short to settle for less.
