**Evening Rituals**

## How a Simple Nightly Reflection Changed My Life

### Focusing on positive moments and creating a calming environment can transform your evenings and set the stage for a more intentional, restful life.

There’s a moment every evening when the day’s noise fades and reflection begins. For many, this quiet brings anxiety—frustrations and regrets replaying in the mind. But I’ve learned that this time can become the foundation for a more peaceful, intentional life. The way you end your day—especially the habits you choose—can transform not just your sleep, but your entire approach to life.

## The Power of an Evening Retrospective

The core of my evening routine is a simple, honest review of my day. Before bed, I ask myself: How did today go? Did I enjoy it? What moments brought me joy, gratitude, or pride? What left me frustrated or dissatisfied?

The goal isn’t to dwell on what went wrong. Instead, I consciously focus on the positive—highlighting experiences that brought happiness, fulfillment, or accomplishment. Giving mental space to these moments sets the stage for restful sleep and a more optimistic outlook.

Why avoid negativity before bed? Ruminating on what didn’t go well can trigger stress and anxiety, dragging up not just today’s disappointments but old, unresolved memories. This mental spiral disrupts rest. By nurturing positive memories and feelings, I help my mind settle peacefully.

## Creating a Calming Environment

Mindset matters, but so does your environment. One of the most effective changes I’ve made is adjusting lighting as evening approaches.

I switch my devices—computer, phone, tablet—to “night mode” or “warm light.” This shifts harsh blue tones to softer yellow, signaling to my brain it’s time to wind down. Sometimes, I even set screens to black and white for extra calm.

If you want to go further, try yellow-tinted glasses. I use them occasionally, though my nearsightedness means I can’t always wear them comfortably. Still, for those who can, they’re a simple way to reduce blue light exposure.

Dimming the room’s lighting is another game-changer. In my apartment, I’ve installed smart LED bulbs—like those from TP-Link or Philips Hue. These bulbs can be programmed to gradually dim at a set hour, creating a gentle transition from day to night. They connect via Wi-Fi and can be controlled with an app, making it easy to tailor the ambiance. Since making this switch, I feel noticeably more relaxed in the evenings.

## Practical Steps for a Restful Night

Here’s how you can put these ideas into practice:

1. **Reflect on Your Day:** Each night, take a few minutes to review your day. Focus on what went well and what made you feel grateful or proud.
2. **Shift to Warm Lighting:** Adjust your device settings to reduce blue light. Use “night mode” or “warm light” features, or switch to grayscale.
3. **Dim Your Environment:** Install smart LED bulbs that can be programmed to dim in the evening. This helps signal to your body that it’s time to wind down.
4. **Try Yellow Glasses:** If you spend a lot of time on screens, yellow-tinted glasses can further reduce blue light exposure.
5. **Incorporate Relaxation Techniques:** I love the “4-4-4” breathing exercise from sophrology. Inhale through your nose for four seconds, hold for four seconds, then exhale for four seconds, letting your abdomen relax. Repeat this cycle five or six times. It’s a simple way to calm the mind and body before sleep.
